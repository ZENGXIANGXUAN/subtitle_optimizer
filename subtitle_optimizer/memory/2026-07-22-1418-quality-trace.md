# 14:18 batch quality trace

## Symptom

The `Live Charting Program 1_chunked - 副本` job completed and exported, but
reported a failed full-document consistency guide, a source-integrity warning,
and 178 non-blocking lint warnings.

## Evidence and root causes

1. **Consistency guide failure is real external infrastructure failure, not a
   subtitle-content false positive.** The first guide-level attempt ended at
   14:15:20 with proxy HTTP 504 after its 165 second budget and three retryable
   upstream failures. The second guide-level attempt ended at 14:18:24 with
   proxy HTTP 502 after 76.688 seconds and five retryable upstream failures.
   The pipeline made both configured guide-level attempts; it retained usable
   translated/optimized text and skipped only the full-document terminology
   audit.

2. **The final source-integrity warning is a false positive.** Integrity passes
   immediately after semantic reflow. `finalize_entries()` then sorts cues by
   timestamp. Two overlapping cues can have timestamp order different from
   original source order, so the order-sensitive signature changes. A local
   reproduction with the job's `AFTER_TRANSLATE` snapshot retained all 1,872
   source-cue signature members (`Counter(expected) == Counter(actual)`) but
   swapped adjacent cues at the first mismatch. This is an ordering-only
   provenance-check failure, not source text loss.

3. **The 84 repairs and 178 warnings are not one class of failure.** The job
   went from 1,872 to 1,807 entries: 65 entries were merged/removed by the
   conservative engineering pass. Of the remaining 19 repair counts, one is
   renumbering and 18 are timing/duration corrections. The 178 warnings are:
   24 large timestamp overlaps, 8 high/critical reading-pressure values,
   145 entries longer than 8 seconds, and one 400 ms cue. The UI summary only
   prints the first three headline categories, which makes the displayed
   `8 + 24` look inconsistent with the total 178.

4. **The final `字幕质检未发现关键提示` log is technically consistent with the
   current non-blocking policy but is misleading.** A skipped consistency audit
   gets `consistency_guide_unapplied`, and the source warning gets
   `source_integrity_diff`; neither is a critical gate flag. It means no
   export-blocking critical issue, not that there were no quality advisories.

## Representative remaining items

- Reading pressure: #38 (400 ms), #255 (41.7 CPS), #624/#646/#656/#723/#1069,
  and #1629.
- Large overlaps include #162 -> #163 (13,996 ms), #313 -> #314 (14,668 ms),
  #964 -> #965 (12,700 ms), and #1792 -> #1793 (9,776 ms). These are actual
  timestamp overlaps in the exported SRT, although they are advisories rather
  than a reason to stop export.
- The long-duration warnings are threshold matches (145 cues, 8.1--14.9 s),
  expected in part from semantic reflow keeping a complete translation over a
  source span; they are not evidence of a translation failure.

## Status

Diagnosis complete; no production code changed. Future fix should make the
integrity check compare membership separately from order after final timestamp
sorting, and make the final log say “no blocking critical issue; N advisories”
rather than imply that no warnings occurred.
